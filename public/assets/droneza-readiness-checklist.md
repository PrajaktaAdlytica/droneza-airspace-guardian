# Drone Incident Readiness Checklist

This checklist is a general planning aid for a fictional Dronezio product demonstration. It is not legal, aviation, security, or regulatory advice.

## Before an incident

- Define who can open, assess, escalate, and close a drone-related incident.
- Maintain current contact and escalation routes.
- Separate observation confidence from potential operational consequence.
- Define approved actions for each role and scenario.
- Establish retention, access, redaction, and evidence-hold policies.
- Test the workflow with a synthetic tabletop exercise.

## During an incident

- Capture exact or estimated time, location, direction, duration, and source.
- Permit “unknown” values instead of forcing certainty.
- Preserve original media and its provenance.
- Link related reports without overwriting individual observations.
- Record confidence, consequence, ownership, rationale, and authorised actions separately.
- Keep one chronological operational timeline.

## After an incident

- Record service impact, closure rationale, and unresolved questions.
- Review access, custody, and disclosure events.
- Export originals, hashes, metadata, and a manifest where appropriate.
- Apply the correct retention or legal-hold policy.
- Assign improvement actions with owners and due dates.
