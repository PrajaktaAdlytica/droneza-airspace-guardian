# Droneza project instructions

- Keep Droneza positioned as workflow software after a drone report. Do not claim detection, identification, tracking, classification, interdiction, certification, deployment, customers, or measured performance.
- Keep all incidents, organisations, records, and operational data explicitly fictional.
- Preserve the approved carbon-blue, warm-white, oxblood, and operational-grey identity.
- Maintain the cinematic incident-entry narrative, restrained motion, reduced-motion fallback, and responsive behavior.
- Preserve the full page set: product overview, Log, Risk, Evidence, solutions, security, company, request demo, sign in, privacy, and terms.
- Remove third-party template branding and avoid reintroducing Lovable assets or dependencies.
- Before publishing, run `npm audit --omit=dev --audit-level=high` and `npm run build:vercel`.
